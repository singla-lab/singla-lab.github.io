Cosine Similarity

EEC 351 · Fundamentals of AI/ML · Prof. Parikshit Pareek & Prof. Jitin Singla · IIT Roorkee

The inner product factorises as x⊤y = ‖x‖2 ‖y‖2 cos θ. Solving for cos θ gives the cosine similarity — a number in [−1, 1] that measures how much two vectors point in the same direction, ignoring their lengths. Drag either arrowhead to change the vectors.

cos θ  =  x⊤y / (‖x‖2 · ‖y‖2)   ∈   [−1, 1]

Interpretation

Where this appears in ML

  • Word/sentence embeddings: the cosine between two embedding vectors measures semantic similarity. "king" and "queen" have high cosine similarity; "king" and "banana" have low.
  • Information retrieval: rank documents by cos(query, document) — independent of how long each document is.
  • Recommender systems: user-item or item-item similarity via cosine.
  • Cauchy-Schwarz is exactly the statement that this quantity stays in [−1, 1] — geometrically obvious, algebraically non-trivial.
  • Not a distance: can be negative, doesn't satisfy the triangle inequality. The "cosine distance" 1 − cos θ patches the first issue.
