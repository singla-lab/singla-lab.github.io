What we work on

Three threads, one question: how do you get from raw measurement to something you can reason about?

All research
01

Cellular architecture

Mitochondrial network modelling with soft X-ray tomography

Soft X-ray tomography (SXT) rapidly maps ultrastructure across whole, intact cells. We build the tools that turn those volumes into measurable biology.
02

AI for healthcare

Non-invasive diagnostics from images and signals

Jaundice affects 60–80% of newborns worldwide. A phone camera should be enough to triage it.
03

Low-resource NLP

AI models for Sanskrit

Sanskrit is morphologically rich and data-poor — exactly the regime where modern NLP is weakest.

Principal Investigator

Jitin Singla, PhD

Assistant Professor · Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering, IIT Roorkee

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering at IIT Roorkee. My research group develops computational frameworks that bridge biology, data science and engineering — specifically, problems in computational biology and AI-driven bioimage analysis for healthcare diagnostics. More recently we have begun an adventure into Sanskrit-AI tools.

Our current projects span computational characterization of cellular architecture, AI-based jaundice detection, ECG analysis, and AI models for Sanskrit.

Divyanshu Bathla awarded the IndiaAI PhD Fellowship

Divyanshu Bathla, a doctoral student in the lab, has received the IndiaAI PhD Fellowship for his work building Sanskrit-AI tools. A national initiative under the IndiaAI Mission, it supports full-time doctoral researchers in AI with a monthly stipend of ₹70,000–80,000, an annual research grant of ₹2 lakh, and conference travel support of up to ₹1 lakh.
