2026 · Research

Two papers accepted in Structure and Journal of Structural Biology

Morphotype-resolved 3D morphometry of mitochondrial networks accepted in Structure, and robust mitochondria segmentation with soft X-ray tomography published in the Journal of Structural Biology.

March 2026 · Award

Divyanshu Bathla awarded the IndiaAI PhD Fellowship

Divyanshu Bathla, a doctoral student in the lab, has received the IndiaAI PhD Fellowship. A national initiative under the IndiaAI Mission, it supports full-time doctoral researchers in AI with a monthly stipend of ₹70,000–80,000, an annual research grant of ₹2 lakh, and conference travel support of up to ₹1 lakh.

6 December 2025 · Outreach

Interaction with participants at MMTTC, HNB Garhwal University

Prof. Parikshit Pareek and Jitin Singla engaged with attendees at MMTTC as part of a Faculty Development Programme on “Harnessing AI in Higher Education for an Innovative Future”.

Computational Biology Conference 2025, RCB Faridabad
