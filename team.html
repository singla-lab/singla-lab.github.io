Principal Investigator

Jitin Singla, PhD

Assistant Professor
Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering, IIT Roorkee
Centre for Indian Knowledge System (Associated Faculty)

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering at IIT Roorkee. My research group develops computational frameworks that bridge biology, data science and engineering — specifically, problems in computational biology and AI-driven bioimage analysis for healthcare diagnostics. More recently we have begun an adventure into Sanskrit-AI tools.

Our current projects span computational characterization of cellular architecture, AI-based jaundice detection, ECG analysis, and AI models for Sanskrit.

I also serve as Scientific Advisor to the World in a Cell project under the World Building Media Lab, where we contribute to data-driven visualization and modelling of cellular systems.

Before joining IIT Roorkee I graduated with a doctorate in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics and an M.S. in Computer Science (High-Performance Computing and Simulation) from the University of Southern California, Los Angeles, and a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Roorkee.
